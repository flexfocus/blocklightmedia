## Stripe products, invoices and payment links — after owner approval

- Fixed packages: **one product per service/package** with its **full price** and a distinct **50% deposit Price**; use the approved **deposit price** in a Payment Link only after date and scope are agreed.
- Remaining balance: prefer a **customer-specific invoice** referencing the prior deposit and agreed total; generic final-balance links do not automatically reconcile a booking.
- True monthly retainer: after agreeing duration, plan and cancellation terms, use a **recurring monthly Stripe Price / subscription or invoice**, not a one-time payment link incorrectly described as a subscription. The customer must explicitly agree to recurring payments.
- Custom quote projects (weddings, commercials, corporate productions, tournaments): **do not publish a fixed checkout**. Send a custom invoice and payment terms after written acceptance.
- Do not connect a payment link to an unapproved calendar reservation or imply payment confirms an unavailable date.
- Stripe's media delivery is not automatically access-controlled; a locked client portal requires secure file storage, payment verification, and customer/project access control.
- Stripe Checkout Form engineering remains a separate **disabled draft** (PR #9); creating price documents does not enable that checkout.

## Approval checklist

1. Six standard photography package prices are approved; confirm durations, number of edits, timelines, usage and cancellation terms. Larger events and commercial work stay custom-quoted.
2. Confirm exact duration, session count, video/photo deliverables, resolution, turnaround, revisions and whether raw files are included.
3. Establish date-approval workflow; contracts, changes/cancellations/refunds, travel radius, second shooter rules and due date for final balances.
4. Confirm legal/account status, sales tax registration and each service's actual tax treatment.
5. Only then map approved **Stripe Price IDs** to their products, create/update deposit Payment Links and link approved packages from the website.
