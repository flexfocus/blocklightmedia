THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Event photography needs to capture the full story: the room, the people, the speakers and the sponsors, all without getting in the way. We work quietly and plan coverage around your run-of-show.

Before the event we review your schedule and identify the must-have moments: keynote speakers, award presentations, VIP arrivals, sponsor signage and group photos. During the event we balance those planned shots with candid images of guests enjoying the experience.

Event images are often needed quickly for press releases, social posts and sponsor reports. Let us know your deadlines and we will plan delivery around them.

What this covers

  • Galas, fundraisers and award nights
  • Brand activations and launches
  • Community and nonprofit events

What you receive
