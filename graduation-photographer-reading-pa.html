THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Graduation portraits should look like the graduate, not a template. We plan sessions around personality, whether that is a sport, a hobby, a favorite place or a classic cap-and-gown look.

A typical session includes a few outfit changes and a couple of locations, such as a campus landmark, a downtown backdrop or a park. Bring the props that matter to you: a jersey, an instrument, a letterman jacket or the diploma itself.

Group sessions with friends or teammates are a fun way to share a session. Families often add a few frames with parents or siblings at the end.

What this covers

  • High school senior portraits
  • College cap-and-gown sessions
  • Friend and teammate group sessions
  • Family frames with the graduate

What you receive

  • Location and wardrobe guidance
  • A relaxed, guided session
  • Edited final images
  • Files ready for announcements and social media

A good fit for

  • High school seniors
  • College and graduate-school grads
  • Student-athletes wanting sport-themed portraits

QUESTIONS

When should we book graduation photos?

Spring and early summer fill quickly. Reach out early with your preferred dates, especially if you need images for printed announcements.

Can we include sports gear or props?

Absolutely. Props and uniforms help show who the graduate is. For athletes, we can also plan an action-style session.
