THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Individual headshots and team sessions for websites, LinkedIn, directories and company marketing.

The most common headshot problem is inconsistency: a team page where every photo was taken in a different place, with different light and a different crop. We set up a repeatable lighting and background setup so every person on your team matches, whether we photograph five people or fifty.

Most people do not enjoy being photographed. We guide posture, expression and angles throughout the session so the result looks confident and natural rather than stiff.

What this covers

  • Individual professionals
  • Leadership teams
  • Staff photo days at your office
  • Matching new hires to an existing look

What you receive

  • Guided posing
  • Consistent lighting
  • Retouching
  • Agreed image formats

A good fit for

  • Companies updating a team or About page
  • Professionals refreshing a LinkedIn profile
  • Firms that need directory-ready portraits

QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

Can you come to our office?

Yes. We can bring a portable lighting and backdrop setup to your workplace in and around Reading, PA so your team does not lose time traveling.

Can you match headshots we already have?

Usually, yes. Send examples of your current headshots and we will plan lighting, background and crop to match as closely as possible.

What should people wear?

Solid colors that fit your company culture tend to work best. We send simple wardrobe guidance before the session.
