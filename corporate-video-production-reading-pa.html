THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Create compelling company profiles, recruiting videos, executive interviews and internal communications that feel elevated rather than generic.

Corporate video often has to satisfy many stakeholders: leadership, HR, marketing and sometimes legal. We keep the process organized with a clear brief, interview questions approved in advance and a defined review round so projects stay on schedule.

Interviews are the backbone of most corporate films. We light and mic every speaker properly and coach them through the conversation so they come across as confident and authentic, then add B-roll of your people and workplace to bring the story to life.

QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

Our team is not comfortable on camera. Can you help?

Yes. We share questions in advance, keep the setup relaxed and guide speakers through the conversation. Most people are far more natural on camera than they expect.

Can you film at our office or facility?

Yes. We bring lighting and audio gear to your location and plan the schedule around your operations.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

Review rounds are defined in your written quote so expectations are clear from the start.
