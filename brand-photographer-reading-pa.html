Editorial-style personal and business branding photography for websites, advertising and social platforms.

Personal branding photography goes beyond a single headshot. It is a library of images that shows who you are, how you work and what it feels like to work with you: you at your desk, with clients, in your workspace, or with the tools of your trade.

We start by understanding your audience and where the images will appear. A coach launching a new website needs different frames than a realtor refreshing social media. The result is a varied, on-brand set you can use for months.

QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

How is this different from a headshot session?

A headshot is one clean portrait. A branding session produces a range of images, including portraits, candid working shots, detail images and locations, built around how you market yourself.

Can we shoot at more than one location?

Yes. Many branding sessions include an office or studio plus an outdoor or lifestyle location. We will plan timing and travel together.

Will you help plan outfits and locations?

Yes. Planning is part of the process. We help you choose wardrobe, locations and props that fit your brand.
