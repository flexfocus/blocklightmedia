THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Tournaments move fast: multiple courts, back-to-back games and big moments happening at the same time. We plan coverage around your schedule so the recap captures the atmosphere, the competition and the people who made it happen.

For organizers, tournament video does more than document. It promotes the next event, gives sponsors visible value and helps attract teams. We plan coverage priorities with you, including championship games, award ceremonies, sponsor activations and crowd energy.

Deliverables can include a hero recap film, daily social clips released during the event, sponsor shout-outs and championship highlights. We will match the plan to your timeline and promotional goals.

  • Championship and award coverage
  • Sponsor and vendor content
  • In-event social clips

What you receive

  • Multi-day production as agreed
