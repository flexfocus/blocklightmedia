THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

We photograph weddings the way they actually feel: candid moments, real emotion and cinematic portraits, without pulling you away from your guests for hours.

Every wedding has a different rhythm. Before the day, we build a timeline with you that covers getting ready, the ceremony, family formals, couple portraits and key reception moments. That plan keeps the day moving and makes sure nothing important is missed.

We pay equal attention to the big moments and the small ones: a parent fixing a tie, the first look, a grandparent on the dance floor. Many couples pair photography with wedding videography so both are planned together.

What this covers

  • Getting-ready coverage
  • Ceremony and family formals
  • Couple portraits
  • Reception, toasts and dancing

What you receive

  • Timeline planning call
  • Coverage for the hours agreed in your quote
  • Edited gallery of final images
  • Online gallery to share with family

A good fit for

  • Couples marrying in Reading and Berks County
  • Intimate weddings and elopements
  • Couples booking photo and video together

Pricing & booking

Every project is quoted individually based on scope, date and location. Request your date; availability and any deposit terms are confirmed in writing before anything is booked.
