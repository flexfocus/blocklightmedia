THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Maternity sessions are about comfort first. We keep the pace slow, guide every pose gently, and leave room for breaks so the portraits feel natural instead of staged.

Many parents choose a session in the third trimester, when the bump is clearly visible but moving around is still comfortable. We will help you pick timing, a location and wardrobe that suit your style, from soft and minimal to bold and editorial.

Partners, siblings and even pets are welcome. A mix of solo, couple and family frames tells a fuller story of this moment.

What this covers

  • Solo maternity portraits
  • Couple and partner portraits
  • Sibling and family frames
  • Outdoor, in-home or location sessions

What you receive

  • Planning guidance on timing and wardrobe
  • A guided, comfortable session
  • Edited, retouched final images
  • Online gallery delivery

A good fit for

  • Expecting parents
  • Families welcoming another child
  • Anyone wanting announcement imagery

QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

When is the best time for a maternity session?

Many parents book between about 28 and 36 weeks, but the right timing is whatever feels comfortable for you. Reach out early so we can hold flexible dates.

What if I need to reschedule?

Pregnancy is unpredictable. Let us know as soon as you can and we will work with you on a new date based on availability.
