THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

A music video should feel like the song. We work with artists to develop a visual concept, whether performance-driven, narrative or experimental, that matches the energy and story of the track.

It starts with listening. We talk through the song, your references and your audience, then build a treatment covering locations, wardrobe, lighting style and a shot list. Clear pre-production is what makes ambitious ideas achievable on an independent budget.

Beyond the full video, we can cut teaser clips, vertical versions and lyric moments for release promotion on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

What this covers

  • Concept and treatment development
  • Performance and narrative filming
  • Lyric, visualizer and promo clips
  • Behind-the-scenes content

What you receive

  • Creative treatment and shot plan
  • Production day(s) as quoted
  • Edited and color-graded music video
  • Teaser and vertical cuts if agreed

A good fit for

  • Independent artists and bands
  • Producers and labels
  • Artists planning a single or album rollout

Pricing & booking

Every project is quoted individually based on scope, date and location. Request your date; availability and any deposit terms are confirmed in writing before anything is booked.
