THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Online shoppers cannot pick up your product, so your photos have to do that work. We produce clear, accurate product images that show shape, texture, scale and detail, plus styled scenes that show the product in use.

Product shoots usually combine two styles. Catalog images use a clean, consistent background so your store or marketplace listing looks organized. Lifestyle images place the product in a real setting to help customers imagine owning it.

Reflective, transparent and very small products each need different lighting. Tell us what you sell and where it will be listed, and we will plan the setup, angles and file formats around your platform requirements.

What this covers

  • White or solid-background catalog images
  • Styled lifestyle and flat-lay scenes
  • Detail and texture close-ups
  • Images for packaging, menus and print

What you receive

  • Shot list built around your product range
  • Consistent lighting across every item
  • Edited, color-corrected images
  • Exports sized for your store or marketplace

A good fit for

  • E-commerce and Shopify brands
  • Makers and local manufacturers
  • Restaurants and food brands needing menu imagery

Pricing & booking

Every project is quoted individually based on scope, date and location. Request your date; availability and any deposit terms are confirmed in writing before anything is booked.
