THE SERVICE

Every detail,
considered.

Most brands do not need one big video. They need a steady stream of strong content. Brand content production gives you planned, recurring shoot days that keep your social channels, ads and website fresh.

We plan content in batches. Before each shoot, we agree on a content calendar: short-form reels, behind-the-scenes clips, product spotlights, team features or promotional pieces. One efficient production day can then cover several weeks of posts.

Working on a recurring basis means we learn your brand, your audience and what performs. Each batch builds on the last, so your content looks consistent and improves over time.

What this covers

  • Short-form vertical video for Reels, TikTok and Shorts
  • Behind-the-scenes and team features
  • Product and service spotlights
  • Supporting photography from the same shoot days

What you receive

  • A content plan for each shoot day
  • Batch production sessions
  • Edited, platform-ready clips
  • Organized delivery for your team or social manager

A good fit for

  • Brands posting consistently on social media
  • Restaurants, gyms and retail businesses
  • Marketing teams without in-house video

Pricing & booking

Every project is quoted individually based on scope, date and location. Request your date; availability and any deposit terms are confirmed in writing before anything is booked.

QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

How does recurring content work?

We agree on a regular schedule, such as monthly, and plan each shoot day in advance. Scope and schedule are outlined in your quote.

Do you post the content for us?

Our focus is planning, filming and editing. We deliver files ready for your team or social media manager to publish.

Can we combine photo and video on the same day?

Yes. Capturing both on the same shoot day is one of the most efficient ways to build a content library.
